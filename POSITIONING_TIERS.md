# Positioning tier criteria

Classify each property as **budget**, **mid-range**, or **boutique** from the live site’s **tone and presentation** — not star ratings or absolute price alone. Price can support a judgment; copy, visual system, and guest promise decide it.

## Decision signals

| Signal | Budget | Mid-range | Boutique |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Voice** | Practical, casual, deal-forward | Warm, family/leisure, clear & hospitable | Curated, intimate, sensory; “home away from home” / experience-led |
| **Promise** | Affordable stay, basics covered | Comfortable self-contained stay with nice extras | Distinctive rooms, design, personal service |
| **Visual system** | Bright, simple, high-contrast CTAs; camping/utility cues OK | Cohesive coastal/nature palette; polished but approachable | Refined typography, restrained color, editorial photography |
| **Typography** | Friendly utilitarian | Readable display + clean body (brand fonts OK) | Expressive serif/display + careful hierarchy |
| **Pricing UI** | Prominent low rates, camping/cabin ladders | Seasonal tables, “from” whole-unit rates, transparent extras | Soft “from” room rates or enquiry-led; less spreadsheet-like |
| **Amenities framing** | Checklist of essentials | Comfort upgrades (kitchen, terrace, hot tub) as lifestyle | Spa, dining, design details as story |
| **Site craft** | Template-forward is fine | Clean customised template / solid WordPress | Highly branded, hotel-product feel |

**Rule of thumb:** If removing hotel-service language still leaves a coherent brand story about place and comfort, prefer **mid-range** over **boutique**. If the site sells beds/pitches and price first, prefer **budget**. Use **boutique** only when tone and presentation consistently signal curated intimacy (not merely a jacuzzi or nice photos).

## Design implications for prototypes

### Budget
- Informal hero; strong price/availability cues early
- Simple booking/enquiry; amenities as practical grid
- Avoid luxury metaphors, gold/navy hotel chrome, sparse editorial layouts

### Mid-range
- Full-bleed nature/place photography; brand name as hero signal
- One clear headline + short supporting line + CTA group
- Show real units and transparent seasonal pricing when the source site does
- Comfort amenities (terrace, grill, hot tub) as lifestyle, not spa theatre
- Polish above the live template without inventing boutique hotel service

### Boutique
- Quiet, editorial first viewport; restrained motion
- Experience-led copy; rooms as curated products
- Soften or de-emphasize dense rate tables; elevate photography and material detail

## Worked example — Silazari Klapkalnciems

**Source:** [https://silazari.lv/](https://silazari.lv/)

**Verdict: mid-range** (coastal family holiday homes — not budget camping, not boutique hotel).

### Tone
- Meta/about: “Vairākus gadus kopts un lolots ģimenes projekts…” — nurtured family project, not luxury brand manifesto
- Unit copy is practical (m², beds, kitchen, AC, playground) with soft nature framing (pine forest, Lāčupītes dendrārijs, 7-minute walk to the sea)
- Jacuzzi section uses warmer language (“klusumā un mierā”, “ekskluzīva iespēja”) but remains a paid add-on lifestyle perk, not a spa program

### Presentation
- WordPress + Bootscore/Bootstrap child theme — competent, not bespoke hotel product design
- Brand palette: deep teal `#062830`, blush/peach `#F7E6DE` / `#E7B49C`, soft mint `#D5EAE4`; logo wordmark in blush on teal context
- Fonts: Granville (display) + Lexend Deca (body) — approachable, not ultra-editorial
- Structure: hero carousel → two houses with seasonal EUR tables → jacuzzi rates → surroundings → contact/WPForms booking

### Why not budget / boutique
- **Not budget:** Private houses, jacuzzi, curated nature story, seasonal premium weekend rates (€150–230/day for the house), polished photography and custom logo
- **Not boutique:** Self-catering holiday homes, spreadsheet-style seasonal pricing, informal family voice, no F&B/concierge/design-hotel framing; “Superior” on OTAs does not override on-site tone

### Prototype direction
Match **mid-range**: coastal full-bleed hero, brand-first, warm teal/blush system, transparent house pricing, comfort amenities as lifestyle — upgrade craft beyond Bootscore without sliding into boutique-hotel chrome.
