Our Venue

A Unique and Inspiring Conference Venue

Nestled in Nottingham's historical Castle Quarter, independently and family run since 1990.

Grand Banquets & Celebrations

Built at the turn of the century and refurbished to give versatility and choice, the Albert Hall is able to host the needs of the modern business world with large global conferences, AGM's, small meetings, exhibitions, fashion shows and corporate dinners.

The more traditional architecture within the venue offers the perfect stage to host concerts, grand banquets, sporting events and examinations followed by the presentation and awards ceremony; the range of events we entertain are limitless.

Whether holding a large international conference, or a small meeting or seminar, the Albert Hall Conference Centre offers the perfect setting to raise the profile of any event. First class service as standard and a venue with a unique wow factor combine to make this the number one choice for conferences in Nottingham and the East Midlands.

Location

Albert Hall Conference Centre — Nottingham, East Midlands, UK

Castle Quarter, Nottingham

The Albert Hall is located in the idyllic Castle Zone part of the City Centre. Some of the architecture in the vicinity is amongst the finest in the City, and this upmarket area is highly desirable — only 2 minutes walk from Nottingham City Centre's vibrant hub, The Old Market Square, yet retaining a peaceful and relaxing ambiance associated with Wellington Circus.

Exceptional transport links and preferential car parking rates are available, along with accommodation from leading hotels, all within a short walking distance.

  • Address North Circus Street, Nottingham, NG1 5AA
  • Area Castle Quarter / Castle Zone
  • Nearby Old Market Square (2 min walk)
  • Nearby St Barnabas Cathedral, Nottingham Playhouse
  • Rail 18 min walk from Nottingham Train Station
  • Air East Midlands Airport via Skylink (45 min)
  • Road M1 Junction 24, 25 or 26
  • Parking Discounted NCP Mount Street & St James Street
