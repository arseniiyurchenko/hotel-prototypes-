A home in Old Rauma

Huoneistohotelli Vanha Rauma offers comfortable, high-quality apartments in the heart of Old Rauma — a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The property sits in an early-1800s estate with classic high ceilings and airy interiors, while every apartment has modern comforts from washing machines to broadband.

Whether you travel alone, as a couple, or with family, stay at least three nights and live as if at home — just steps from cobbled streets, cafés, and craft shops.

Find us

Location

In the heart of Old Rauma

The apartment hotel is located on Kauppakatu in Vanha Rauma (Old Rauma), a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1991 — about 50 metres from Kauppatori market square.

Step outside to craft workshops, cafés, restaurants, and museums including Rauman museo, Rauman taidemuseo, and Rauman Merimuseo.

  • Address Kauppakatu 18, 26100 Rauma
  • Landmark Kauppatori (~50 m)
  • Nearby Rauman museo · Taidemuseo · Merimuseo
  • Phone 050 078 5304
  • Email info@vanharauma.com
