Ragaciems

By the Baltic Sea

A fishing village on Latvia’s west coast — sand beach, fish market, and lake nature nearby.

Four minutes’ walk to the beach

Arnicāni sits in Ragaciems, about 300 metres from the Baltic Sea shore and 350 metres from Kaņieris Lake. Around you: a tidy residential area, the Ragaciems fish market, a fish restaurant, cafés, and shops.

  • Address LV-3118 Ragaciems, Latvia
  • Beach ~300 m to Baltic Sea
  • Lake Kaņieris (~350 m) — birds, fishing, boat base
  • Nearby Ragaciems sedumi; Lapmežciems museum (~3 km)
