Dumfriesshire, Scotland

Near Moffat, on the Scotland–England border

Only an hour from Glasgow and Edinburgh, Auchen Castle sits on a peaceful country estate close to the border — a tranquil setting away from city distractions.

  • Beattock, Near Moffat, Dumfriesshire DG10 9SH
  • Just off the M74 (Junction 15 from the south; Junction 16 from the north)
  • Nearest station: Lockerbie (about a 20-minute taxi)
  • Edinburgh and Glasgow airports around an hour’s drive
  • Moffat town centre about 10 minutes by car
