At the tip of Latvia

Forest, meadow, and the restless sea — with quiet that stays with you.

Brīvdienu Kolka sits in Kolka village, Kurzeme — very close to Kolkasrags (Cape Kolka), where the Baltic Sea meets the Gulf of Riga.

Address listed on the site: Vīnkalni, Vārpas, Kolka, Latvia. Cottages are offered across two addresses nearby.

  • 1Kolkasrags — about 3 min by car (listed on site)
  • 2Kolkas bāka (lighthouse) — about 10 min on foot
