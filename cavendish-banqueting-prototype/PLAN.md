### Location

Cavendish Banqueting, Edgware Road (opp Mercedes Benz), Colindale, London, NW9 5AE.

Nearby / access (from location pages): Colindale & Burnt Oak underground (≈10 min walk); buses 32 & 142; A5 Edgware Road / M1 / Central London by car; Mill Hill Broadway train + bus 142.

### Hall / space types (as labeled)

1. **Grand Hall** — over 8,000 sq ft; “in excess of 350” guests; FAQ also states venue accommodates up to **400** guests; air-conditioned; AV; adjustable dance floor & stage; neutral décor adaptable with lighting/fabrics.
2. **Bridal room** — dedicated space for bride and party.
3. Site marketing copy refers to “chic & customizable venues” (plural) but only **Grand Hall** and **bridal room** are named specifically on the pages reviewed.

### Event types referenced on site / cost calculator

Weddings & receptions, engagement, Bar/Bat Mitzvah, birthday/anniversary, dinner & dance, Mehendi/Sangeet, Haldi, corporate events, civil ceremony, fashion shows, banquets, concerts. Strong positioning as an **Asian wedding venue in London**.

### Amenities / features (verified on source)

- In-house catering + partner caterers (incl. vegan, halal, kosher specialists)
- Bar service upon request; welcome drinks options in calculator
- Adjustable dance floor & stage; cutting-edge facilities / AV / mood lighting
- Decor packages (calculator lists chiavari chairs, flower walls, mandaps, themes, LED dance floor, etc.)
- Seamless / in-house event coordination & personalized service
- Parking: forecourt spaces (reservable for hosts); **500+ spaces within a 5-minute walk**; “500 Car Parking Spaces Available 24/7” on calculator page
- Licensed until midnight (council approval to extend)
- Indoor sparkler fireworks / fog machine listed as optional extras in calculator

### Pricing / packages

**No fixed £ package prices published** on the homepage or cost calculator results UI. Instant online quote / cost calculator at `/costcalculator/` collects event type, day, month, hours, guest bands, cuisine, bar, décor, etc. Prototype uses inquiry CTA + “Get an instant online quote” language only — no invented prices.

## Design direction

Premium London banqueting: Marcellus display + clean sans body; black + champagne gold from logo; soft stone backgrounds (not cream/terracotta template); full-bleed hero; restrained motion (header, reveal, hero drift).
