The space

The Grand Hall

Decorated in neutral colours and adaptable with lighting and fabrics. The versatile, air-conditioned Grand Hall is fully equipped with audiovisual facilities, an adjustable dance floor, and a stage.

A dedicated bridal room allows the bride and her party to prepare for the reception. Cavendish Banqueting works with clients from all cultures and hosts weddings, birthdays, fashion shows, banquets, and more.

Our key features

Everything for an unforgettable event

Amenities and services described on the live Cavendish Banqueting site.

Exquisite culinary journey

In-house catering teams create menus for your day, with partner specialists available including vegan, halal, and kosher.

Bar upon request

Bar service is available on request for your event day, with welcome drink options via the online quote tools.

Dance floor & stage

An adjustable dance floor and stage give flexibility for receptions, performances, and first dances.

Decor & lighting

Neutral hall adaptable with fabrics and lighting; décor options include flower walls, mandaps, LED dance floor, chiavari chairs, and themed packages.

Event coordination

Seamless planning from concept to execution, with attentive waitstaff and event managers.

Parking nearby

Forecourt spaces can be reserved for hosts; over 500 spaces within a five-minute walk, noted as available 24/7.
