Easy accommodation

Relaxed stays beside Old Rauma

Hotel CityHovi offers relaxed and cozy accommodation in the centre of Rauma. We are a small family hotel with 18 clean, well-furnished rooms — a friendly base whether you arrive by car or by bus.

We sit next to idyllic Old Rauma and along the Rauma canal. The city theatre, library, restaurants, and cafés are just around the corner. Free parking is available on the hotel rooftop.

  • 18 well-furnished rooms with private bathrooms
  • Breakfast buffet always included in the room rate
  • Traditional Finnish sauna available Mon–Sat by request
  • Non-smoking hotel, also suitable for guests with allergies

Find us

Next to Old Rauma

Hotel CityHovi is at Nortamonkatu 18, 26100 Rauma. The hotel address is on Nortamonkatu; the entrance is from Pakkahuoneenkatu. Free parking is on the hotel rooftop (entry from Pakkahuoneenkatu).

We are beside UNESCO World Heritage Old Rauma and the Rauma canal. Rauma’s temporary bus station on Savilankatu is just around the corner.

  • UNESCO Old Rauma
  • Rauma canal
  • City theatre & library
  • Restaurants & cafés nearby
  • Rauma Lace Week / Night of Black Lace
