Exclusive use · Cotswold countryside

A day of uninterrupted space and freedom to think

Situated within 1400 acres of rolling countryside, Deer Park Hall offers elegantly restored Cotswold stone buildings overlooking the Deer Park — where red and fallow deer roam freely — and Stag Oak Vineyard.

Perfect for weddings and impressive for corporate & business events. The grounds and beautifully restored venue are yours for the day.

The Bredon Room — panoramic views to the Deer Park & Malvern Hills

Event & meeting spaces

Rooms designed for today’s conferencing — and evenings that linger

Flexible conference and breakout spaces for seminars, workshops and celebrations, with natural light and beautiful grounds beyond the glass.

The Bredon Room

Largest room at Deer Park Hall, with exposed oak beams, a wall of glass, air conditioning and the Rut Bar within the space. Commonly used for wedding breakfasts and conference theatre layouts.

Up to 150 dining PA system L 18.7m · W 9.5m · H 7.0m

The Panelled Hall

A baronial oak-panelled hall for indoor civil ceremonies, reception drinks, dancing — and U-shaped meeting setups for business gatherings.

Oak panelling Open fire L 10.2m · W 5.0m · H 6.3m

Boardroom & lounges

A professional boardroom for small meetings seating up to six, plus three lounge areas, the Minstrels’ Gallery and outdoor pavilions including a heated pavilion seating up to 12.

Boardroom up to 6 3 lounge areas 2 rustic pavilions

Also on site: Bridal Lounge, a separate kids’ room, Italian and Lavender gardens, terraced lawns, viewing platform and Lower Terrace with outdoor sofas and fire pit.

Conferences & events

Cotswold stone buildings, modern conference kit

No stuffy hotel rooms with poor lighting — instead natural light, air conditioning, audio visual equipment, high-speed Wi-Fi and grounds to revitalise your delegates. Flexibility is key; the friendly team works with you to create a memorable event.

Exclusive use

The grounds and restored venue are yours for the day — uninterrupted space for corporate gatherings, team days and celebrations.

AV & connectivity

Audio visual equipment, high-speed Wi-Fi and air conditioning across conference-ready rooms with abundant natural light.

Licensed bar

The Rut Bar is fully stocked, with award-winning Deer Park Vineyard wines and a specialist gin offering for evening events.

Parking & access

Plenty of on-site parking, good motorway access and a central England location for delegates travelling in.

Gardens & fire-pits

Extensive gardens with outdoor furniture including sofas and fire-pits — ideal for breaks, drinks and informal sessions outdoors.

Wine tours for teams

Team-building days can include vineyard tours and tastings at Stag Oak Vineyard, with drinks served in the Bredon Room, gardens or Oak Pavilion.

The Deer Park & Stag Oak Vineyard

One of the most distinctive settings in the Cotswolds

The grounds overlook the Deer Park where red and fallow deer roam freely, and Stag Oak Vineyard where award-winning English wines are grown.

Enter through a Cotswold stone and redbrick arch, past the Italian garden and Lavender Garden, to terraced lawns framed by countryside — a popular choice for outdoor ceremonies, photographs, canapés and reception drinks.
