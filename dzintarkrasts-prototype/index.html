Atrašanās vieta

Žocene, Gulf of Riga

Atpūtas un sporta komplekss „Dzintarkrasts” sits on the Gulf of Riga coast in Žocene (Rojas pagasts), in a forest setting beside the dune zone — one of Kurzeme’s popular rural tourism places for family rest, celebrations, sports, and corporate gatherings.

Nearby Roja offers a harbour, stone piers, quiet sandy beaches, and coastal nature. The complex name Dzintarkrasts means Amber Coast.

  • Žocene, Rojas pagasts, Talsu novads, LV-3264
  • Gulf of Riga / Baltic Sea coast
  • Near Roja — harbour, beaches, and coastal heritage
  • Forest and dune landscape around the complex
