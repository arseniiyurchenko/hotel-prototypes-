Built 1506 · Grade II* listed

A 16th-century barn on a scheduled monument heritage site

The Great Barn at Headstone Manor and Museum combines the grandeur, charm and character of a 16th century building with 21st century luxuries.

Nestled in picturesque grounds with a further three historic listed buildings, landscaped gardens and a 14th-century moat, the barn offers a vaulted ceiling, exposed wooden oak beams and limestone flooring.

Wedding & special occasion packages

What venue hire includes

Drawn from the packages published on the Weddings and Special Occasions pages. Exclusive barn hire runs 10.00–23.00 Monday to Wednesday and 10.00–23.55 Thursday to Sunday.

  • Dedicated Events Coordinator and duty managers
  • Tables and 200 Chiavari chairs
  • Uplighters and fairy lights throughout the space
  • Sound equipment for background music and speeches
  • Access to the museum grounds for photos
  • Outdoor grassland for marquee / outdoor use
  • Dressing room
  • Finishing kitchen with hot cupboards, fridges and hot water (for plating, not cooking)
  • Catering tent — fixed outdoor structure for all cooking
  • Public car park
  • No corkage fee
  • Easels, décor dividers and ceremony / welcome tables (weddings)

The venue recommends caterers, photographers, florists, entertainment and other suppliers familiar with the site. Detailed conference AV kit beyond sound equipment for music and speeches is not listed on the Corporate page reviewed.
