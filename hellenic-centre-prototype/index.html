Amenities & features

What your booking includes and offers

Features listed across the venue hire pages and 2025 brochure — no on-site car park; nearby options are noted below.

In-house catering

Light breakfasts, working lunches and canapé receptions for up to 100 guests. Preferred caterers for larger dinners and receptions; external catering and corkage subject to fees.

AV & conference kit

Integrated AV in the Great Hall and Friends Room; portable projector, screen and flipchart packs on request for meeting rooms. Modular stage available in the Great Hall.

WiFi

Complimentary WiFi on a dedicated fibre-optic line with high upload and download speeds. Network details provided on the day.

Access

Main entrance via steps; a stair climber is available with prior notice. Lift to all floors except the third. Disabled-access toilets on the lower ground floor. Hearing induction loop in the Great Hall. Assistance animals welcome.

Bar & cloakroom

Bar area can be hired exclusively with the Great Hall for receptions. Cloakroom on the lower ground floor; attendants can be arranged.

Parking nearby

No off-street parking or loading bays at the building. Metered / pay & display nearby; Euro Car Parks (Marylebone Square, Aybrook Street) and NCP Marylebone Road listed on the site. Blue Badge bays on nearby streets.

Venue hire income supports the Centre’s programme of free public events. Corporate Membership includes venue hire discounts. Bookings team: bookings@helleniccentre.org · 020 7563 9834.

Cultural programme

A hub for Hellenic culture in London

Lectures, exhibitions, concerts, social events and Greek language courses — promoting awareness of Hellenic culture and nurturing UK relations with the Hellenic world. Founded as a charity in 1994.
