Over 700 years of celebrations

Hever Castle has played host to many important events and celebrations for over 700 years. Today the Astor Wing, together with the Guthrie Pavilion in the Italian Garden and areas of the Castle itself, are available for corporate and private events — each offering peace, privacy and seclusion.

Castle rooms · up to 60 ceremony · Great Hall breakfast for 40

The Castle

The romantic double-moated castle was once the childhood home of Anne Boleyn. Two rooms can be hired for a castle wedding: the Inner Hall for ceremonies and the Great Hall for a wedding breakfast, with evening options including the Moat Restaurant, tipi on the Tudor Lawn, or the Guthrie Pavilion on selected dates.

Astor Wing · exclusive use · Tudor Suite · up to 60 guests

The Astor Wing

A charming Edwardian wing designed in Tudor style for William Waldorf Astor in 1903, with oak panelling, ornate fireplaces and antique furnishings. The Tudor Suite Sitting Room and Dining Room each accommodate up to 60 guests; drinks may be taken in the Music Room or on the Tudor Suite Terrace and lawns. Stretch tent, tipi or Guthrie Pavilion options extend evening capacity.

Italian Garden · Loggia · Guthrie Pavilion · up to 220 evening

Italian Garden & Guthrie Pavilion

Ceremonies under the arched columns of the lakeside Loggia, drinks on the Loggia Terrace, then a wedding breakfast in the Palladian-style Guthrie Pavilion — accommodating up to 180 for dinner and up to 220 for the evening reception. The pavilion also serves as a conference and event venue for groups of up to 200 delegates.

Amenities & features

Drawn from Hever Castle’s venue hire, weddings and corporate hospitality pages — only what the live site states.

Event coordination

Event co-ordinators for hire enquiries via events@hevercastle.co.uk or 01732 861800. Wedding planners support wedding bookings.

Catering

In-house team of chefs; seasonal menus using fresh and, when possible, local produce from the Garden of England.

Accommodation

26 bedrooms graded five stars by Quality in Tourism in the Astor Wing and Anne Boleyn Wing — period features with modern comfort. Minimum of ten bedrooms when bedrooms form part of an event. Medley Court holiday cottage also available on the estate.

Gardens & grounds

Award-winning gardens set in 125 acres, including the Italian Garden, rose gardens, formal lawns, lake and Loggia.

Historic architecture

Double-moated castle, panelled rooms, Minstrels Gallery, Boleyn coat of arms fireplace, oak panelling and William Waldorf Astor’s Italian sculpture collection.

Private parking

Private car park for Castle wedding venue parties, accessed via the private gated entrance to the Astor Wing. General visitor parking details are not listed on the venue-hire page.

Corporate facilities

Championship golf course, wellbeing centre, activities field, outdoor heated swimming pool and tennis court available to combine with corporate hospitality.

Sustainability

Silver rated for green tourism — local produce where possible, biomass boiler and solar panels across the estate.
