Over 700 years of celebrations

Hever Castle has played host to many important events and celebrations for over 700 years. Today the Astor Wing, together with the Guthrie Pavilion in the Italian Garden and areas of the Castle itself, are available for corporate and private events — each offering peace, privacy and seclusion.

Venues with unique character

Spaces labelled across Hever Castle’s venue hire and wedding pages: the Castle, the Astor Wing, the Italian Garden with Guthrie Pavilion, and Lord Astor’s Study.

Castle rooms · Inner Hall ceremony for 60 · Great Hall breakfast for 40

The Castle

The romantic double-moated castle was once the childhood home of Anne Boleyn. Two rooms can be hired for a castle wedding: the Inner Hall for ceremonies and the Great Hall for a wedding breakfast. Evening options include the Moat Restaurant, a tipi on the Tudor Lawn, or the Guthrie Pavilion on selected dates.

Astor Wing · Tudor Suite · up to 60 guests · exclusive use options

The Astor Wing

Added by William Waldorf Astor when restoring the castle, the Astor Wing offers Tudor-style spaces with oak panelling and antique furnishings. The Tudor Suite Sitting Room and Dining Room each accommodate up to 60 guests; drinks may be taken in the Music Room or on the Tudor Suite Terrace and lawns. Evening options include a tipi or stretch tent, a sailcloth marquee on the Winter Garden Lawn, or the Guthrie Pavilion on certain dates. For corporate hire, the Astor Wing meeting space suits up to 60 delegates.

Italian Garden · Loggia · Guthrie Pavilion · up to 220 evening

Italian Garden & Guthrie Pavilion

Ceremonies under the arched columns of the lakeside Loggia, drinks on the Loggia Terrace, then a wedding breakfast in the Palladian-style Guthrie Pavilion — up to 180 for dinner and up to 220 for the evening reception. For corporate events, the pavilion accommodates up to 200 delegates as a conference or dinner venue in the award-winning Italian Garden.

Corporate & intimate gatherings · up to 12 guests

Lord Astor’s Study

Listed among Hever Castle’s corporate hospitality venues: a characterful room behind a discreet doorway from the Library, with bookcases, hidden cupboards and Astor’s collection of crystal balls. Accommodates up to 12 guests on one table for intimate gatherings and celebrations.

Amenities & features

Drawn from Hever Castle’s venue hire, weddings and corporate hospitality pages — only what the live site states.

Event coordination

Event co-ordinators for hire enquiries via events@hevercastle.co.uk or 01732 861800. Wedding enquiries via the weddings team at weddings@hevercastle.co.uk.

Catering

In-house team of chefs; seasonal menus using fresh and, when possible, local produce from the Garden of England.

Accommodation

26 bedrooms graded five stars by Quality in Tourism — period colours, panelled walls, vaulted ceilings and en-suite facilities with modern comfort. Minimum of ten bedrooms when bedrooms form part of an event. Medley Court holiday cottage is also available on the estate.

Gardens & grounds

Award-winning gardens set in 125 acres, including the Italian Garden, rose gardens, formal lawns, lake and Loggia.

Historic architecture

Double-moated castle, panelled rooms, Minstrels Gallery, Boleyn coat of arms fireplace, oak panelling and William Waldorf Astor’s Italian sculpture collection.

Private parking

Private car park for Castle wedding venue parties, accessed via the private gated entrance to the Astor Wing. General visitor parking details are not listed on the venue-hire page.

Corporate facilities

Championship golf course, wellbeing centre, activities field for outdoor pursuits, outdoor heated swimming pool and tennis court — available to combine with corporate hospitality.

Sustainability

Silver rated for green tourism — local produce where possible, biomass boiler and solar panels across the estate.
