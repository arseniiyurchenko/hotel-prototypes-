Location

Next to idyllic Old Rauma — easy to reach by car or bus.

Beside Old Rauma & the canal

CityHovi sits in the centre of Rauma beside the UNESCO World Heritage site Old Rauma and along the Rauma canal. The city theatre, library, restaurants, and cafés are just around the corner.

Rauma’s bus station is nearby on Savilankatu. Free parking is on the hotel rooftop (entry from Pakkahuoneenkatu), which is also the hotel entrance street.
