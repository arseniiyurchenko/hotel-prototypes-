Location

In the heart of Vidzeme, near Cesvaine — set in a historic manor park.

Grašu muiža · Cesvaine area

The elegant Grašu manor house, built in 1784 and surrounded by parkland, stands in the centre of Vidzeme near Cesvaine. Guests can enjoy quiet walks among ancient trees in the manor park and visit nearby Cesvaine town, its castle, and park.

Hotel Grašu pils is located in Cesvaines pagasts, Madonas novads (Madona Municipality), Latvia.

  • Address Hotel Grašu Pils, Cesvaines pagasts, Madonas novads, LV-4871, Latvija
