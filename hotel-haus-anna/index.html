Where you’ll stay

Steps from Old Rauma

Haus Anna sits at Satamakatu 7, a short walk from the UNESCO World Heritage wooden town of Old Rauma. Wander market streets, find a café, then return to a quiet, home-like evening upstairs.

Check-in from 3:00 PM; check-out by noon. An extra bed can be added for €20 per night; a baby crib is available free of charge.
