Rauma, Finland

Steps from Old Rauma

Quiet street setting, city-center convenience, and UNESCO wooden blocks a short walk away.

Hotel Kalliohovi sits on a quiet street just a few steps from the city center. The wooden blocks of Old Rauma — with cafés, museums, and boutiques — are only a short walk from the hotel. Street-side rooms and balconies look toward the hundred-year-old Maritime Museum.

  • Old Rauma

    UNESCO World Heritage wooden town — a short walk from the hotel.

  • Maritime Museum

    Visible from street-side rooms and Balcony King balconies.

  • City center

    Restaurants, cafés, and everyday services within easy walking distance.
