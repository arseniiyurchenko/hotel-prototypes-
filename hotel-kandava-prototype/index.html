Find us

Location

Nestled in the heart of historic Kandava, Viesnīca Kandava combines traditional Latvian charm with modern comfort in a beautifully restored building. The hotel sits in the very center of town, close to stores, cafés, and places of interest.

  • Address Sabiles iela 3, Kandava, LV-3120, Latvia
  • Setting Historic center of Kandava
  • Reception Mon–Thu & Sun 10:00–20:00 · Fri–Sat 10:00–21:00
