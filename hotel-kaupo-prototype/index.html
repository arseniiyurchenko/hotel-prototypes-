Location

As described on the Hotel Kaupo site — one of the most beautiful places in Latvia, near Sigulda.

Jaunbeites, 51.km, Sigulda

Hotel Kaupo is a small but very cozy hotel located near Sigulda. Guest feedback on the site mentions it as a strong base for traveling through the nearby national park and for overnight stays when visiting Sigulda.

  • Area Near Sigulda, Latvia
  • Nature Nearby national park (guest review on source site)
  • Train ~3 min by car from Sigulda station
  • Bus ~3 min / ~2 km from Sigulda bus stop
  • Airport ~1 hour from Riga International (RIX)

Note: “Gauja river valley” / “Gauja National Park” by name were not used as marketing claims on the source homepage; Sigulda and a nearby national park are the landmarks referenced there.
