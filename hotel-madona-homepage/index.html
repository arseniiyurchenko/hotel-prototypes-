Location

In the historic Market Square of Madona — a gateway to the Vidzeme highlands.

Heart of Madona

Hotel Madona sits on Saieta laukums in the centre of Madona, with the town’s sights, infrastructure, and sports facilities within easy reach. The surrounding Latvian countryside opens onto lakes and rolling hills often associated with the Vidzeme highlands.

Nearby places highlighted on the hotel’s site include Lake Lubāns, Gaiziņkalns, Cesvaine Palace, Kalsnava Arboretum, and Smeceres Sils recreation centre.
