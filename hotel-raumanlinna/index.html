Rauma

In the heart of the canal city

A quiet street near the centre — Old Rauma’s UNESCO wooden town is a short walk away.

Kalliokatu 25, 26100 Rauma

Hotel Kalliohovi sits on a peaceful street close to the city centre. From street-side rooms and balconies, views open toward the Maritime Museum — over a hundred years old.

  • Old Rauma (Vanha Rauma) Idyllic wooden blocks with cafés, museums, and boutiques — a short walk.
  • Maritime Museum Visible from street-side rooms and Balcony King balconies.
  • Restaurant Kapteeninhuone Marine-atmosphere dining and breakfast on site.
