Location

Kalnjāņi, near Sigulda

Viesnīca Santa is a small suburban hotel in Sigulda municipality (Siguldas novads), Latvia. The hotel helps guests plan time in Sigulda — including castle visits and boat trips on the Gauja river.

Note: The live site does not explicitly name “Gauja National Park.” Location copy here sticks to landmarks the site itself mentions: Sigulda and the Gauja river.
