Location

In the town of nine hills

Welcome to Hotel Talsi in Talsi — a small town of Northern Kurzeme. The hotel sits in a picturesque place next to Vilkmuižas ezers, beside the protected nature area Talsu Pauguraine (Natura 2000).

Enjoy the rest in the 9 hill town — a calm base for exploring Talsi’s hills, lake shores, and local sights. Tourism information: talsitourism.lv · What to do: visittalsi.com

  • Kareivju iela 16, Talsi, Talsu novads LV-3201
  • Next to Vilkmuižas ezers
  • Talsu Pauguraine nature area nearby
