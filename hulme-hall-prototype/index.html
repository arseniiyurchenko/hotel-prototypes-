A versatile Grade II listed hall for hire

Hulme Hall is a versatile building in a prime location, with its own large private car park — suited to weddings, celebrations, corporate meetings, and conferences.

Boasting four event rooms of different sizes, all on one level, giving ten thousand square feet in total for your own self use if you require. Port Sunlight village lies within easy reach of the motorway network and the main A41 road, with a number of hotels close by.

Weddings

Your reception in Port Sunlight

Hulme Hall is a licensed wedding venue and offers the ideal choice of setting for your wedding reception. Set in the unique and picturesque village of Port Sunlight, the charm of the hall & village is unrivalled.

The size of the four function rooms means all numbers can be catered for. On your initial visit, the team will take you around the hall explaining the use of each room. Once confirmed, an in-depth appointment discusses individual ideas and requirements.
