Location

Mazirbe, Baltijas jūras krasts

Jūras mājas “Dzelmes” are in Mazirbe, on the Baltic Sea coast in Kurzeme, within the territory of Slītere National Park. Nearby Livonian villages share local traditions and history of the Livonian people.

Forest trails and bike routes in Slītere National Park, pinewood air, and the quiet Kurzeme seaside are part of the stay described on the source site.

  • Mazirbe, Kolkas pagasts, Dundagas novads
  • Slītere National Park
  • Baltic Sea / Kurzeme seaside
  • Nearby Livonian villages
