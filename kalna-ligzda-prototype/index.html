Location

Historic Kalnamuiža on the edge of Smiltene, Vidzeme — a short climb from town into parkland and manor heritage.

Kalnamuiža-7, Smiltene parish

The hotel sits in the Kalnamuiža territory where Smiltene’s history began — near the German Order castle ruins, wooden manor buildings, and a park that feels like a fairy-tale land in every season. The town centre is close; on foot, guests climb a staircase of 120 steps up the hill.

  • Smiltene — town centre nearby (Vidzeme region)
  • Vidus ezers — manor complex on the steep lakeside bank
  • Castle ruins — German Sword Brothers Order (from 1370)
  • Cheese house — historic sierūzis, Anno 1763
  • “Pa firsta Līvena takām” — Prince Paul von Liven heritage trail
