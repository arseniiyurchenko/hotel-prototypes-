### Location

Kilkenny Castle, The Parade, Kilkenny, R95 YRK1, Ireland. Centre of Kilkenny City; 15-minute walk from McDonagh Railway Station. Nearby named on site: Ireland’s Medieval Mile, Ireland’s Ancient East, Butler Trail; parking near the Castle but not on site; limited coach parking on Castle Road. Sat Nav: 52.65000512443783, −7.249482497622694.

### Event / room spaces (from /venues/)

1. **The Parade Tower** — conferences, receptions, gala dinners, cultural events, civil & humanist ceremonies (theatre 120 / banquet 120 / reception 130)
2. **The Duchess Boardroom** — smaller ceremonies, private dining, meetings, breakout (theatre 20 / banquet 18 / reception 20); wedding ceremonies up to 25 guests
3. **The Ormonde Boardroom** — private dining, smaller meetings, smaller receptions
4. **The Medieval Room** — evening receptions / catering area for conferences (reception 60)
5. **The Picture Gallery** — pre-dinner drinks, evening cultural events (theatre/reception 120)
6. **Park & Gardens / grounds** — outdoor events considered case-by-case (FAQ); formal terraced rose garden, woodlands, lake, ~50 acres / 21 hectares parkland

### Amenities / features (verified on site)

Approved catering partners (listed on Catering page); complimentary Wi-Fi; cloakroom for events; AV (projection booth, sound, screen, laser projector, Barco ClickShare, HDMI, blackout, atmospheric lighting); dedicated events team for viewings and coordination; accessibility information and free admission policy for visitors with disabilities (visitor context); period-room tours for confirmed event delegates; tearoom / playground / orienteering (visitor park amenities).

**Parking:** near Castle but not on site (Find Us).

### Pricing / packages

- **Venue hire rates / wedding packages:** not published on the source site — enquiry only (`kilkennycastleevents@opw.ie`).
- **Visitor admission (Tickets & Times, 2026):** Self-guided Adult €8, Senior €6, Child/Student €4, Family €20; Guided Adult €12, Senior €10, Child/Student €6, Family €30. Children under 12 free; Heritage Card holders free.

## Design direction

Premium historic venue: deep teal (`#004d44`) + burgundy (`#800020`) + limestone greys; display serif + refined sans; atmospheric stone/park photography as visual anchor; restrained motion (hero reveal, scroll fades, header solidify). Not a hotel booking site — event enquiry–led.
