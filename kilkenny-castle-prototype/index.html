Venue Hire

The Perfect Setting for Business or Pleasure

The Parade Tower Wing is the dedicated events area of Kilkenny Castle — the entire west wing and two of its medieval towers — with the Picture Gallery available for evening cultural events and pre-dinner drinks.

The Parade Tower

Theatre 120 Banquet 120 Reception 130

Where old and new are integrated: state-of-the-art facilities in a medieval tower, with full blackout, atmospheric lighting, AV readiness, and open Wi-Fi. Open pine trussed ceiling, tiered balconies, antique marble fireplace, Kilkenny limestone and Irish oak flooring.

  • Gala dinners
  • Conferences
  • Receptions
  • Civil & humanist ceremonies

The Duchess Boardroom

Theatre 20 Banquet 18 Weddings up to 25

Victorian boardroom with elegant gilded plasterwork on the second floor of the Parade Tower wing — intimate surroundings with views over Kilkenny City and the formal Rose Garden.

  • Smaller ceremonies
  • Private dining
  • Boardroom & cabaret meetings
  • Conference breakout

The Ormonde Boardroom

Theatre 15 Banquet 12 Reception 15

A smaller Victorian boardroom on the first floor of the Parade Tower Wing — an exclusive setting for private dining, smaller meetings, and receptions.

  • Private dining
  • Smaller meetings
  • Breakout space
  • Smaller receptions

The Medieval Room

Reception 60 Opens to courtyard

At the base of the Parade Tower: arrow loops and a central oak column supporting a 15th-century post and beam ceiling — an atmospheric space for evening receptions or as a catering area for conferences.

  • Evening receptions
  • Catering for conferences

The Picture Gallery

Theatre 120 Reception 120 East wing

Nineteenth-century gallery built for the Butler family’s paintings — an elegant setting for pre-dinner drinks before dinner at The Parade Tower, and for evening cultural and artistic events.

  • Pre-dinner drinks
  • Evening cultural events

Park & Gardens: the Castle stands in rolling parkland with a formal terraced rose garden, woodlands and lake. Outdoor events on the grounds are considered case-by-case via the Events Office.

Features

What the Castle provides for your event

Drawn from the Venue Hire, Catering, Weddings, and FAQ pages on kilkennycastle.ie.

Events team

Experienced and dedicated events team for viewings, provisional bookings, and liaison on the finer details.

Catering partners

All catering via approved providers listed on the Catering page — from light lunches to gala dinners and drinks receptions.

Complimentary Wi‑Fi

Open Wi‑Fi throughout the complex (KKC_Guest), including The Parade Tower.

Audio visual

Projection booth, sound system, screen, laser projector with wireless Barco ClickShare, cabled HDMI, full blackout and atmospheric lighting.

Ceremony setting

For wedding ceremonies: theatre-style setting, top table, white chair covers, red aisle carpet, lectern, AV technician and full AV services.

Cloakroom

Cloakroom facilities are available for events.

Delegate tours

Once an event is confirmed, visits to the period rooms can be arranged for your delegates.

Parkland setting

About fifty acres of parkland with mature trees, wildlife, formal rose garden, woodlands and lake.

Accessibility

Kilkenny Castle and Park welcome all visitors and publish accessibility guidance; the historic fabric presents access challenges, with improvements ongoing.

Find Us

In the heart of Kilkenny

Located at the very heart of Kilkenny, within easy walking distance of many other attractions and amenities. McDonagh Railway Station is about a 15-minute walk. Parking is available near the Castle but not on site; limited coach parking is available on Castle Road.

  • Ireland’s Medieval Mile
  • Ireland’s Ancient East
  • The Butler Trail
  • City centre amenities within walking distance
