Rauma archipelago

How to reach the island

Bothnian Sea National Park, off Rauma

Kuuskajaskari is the largest island in Rauma’s central archipelago — about 30 hectares, rising up to 15 metres above sea level, roughly four kilometres from the mainland.

Reach it by scheduled or charter water bus with Rauman Saaristokuljetus (M/V Apollonia & M/V Linnea). Home port: Marina Vista, Suvitie 14, 26100 Rauma. Remember to book ferry tickets separately when you reserve a cottage.
