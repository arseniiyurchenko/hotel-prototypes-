Rauma archipelago

Location & access

Kylmäpihlaja is the outermost of the large islands off Rauma and part of Selkämeri National Park.

Arrive by ferry to a lighthouse island

Reach Kylmäpihlaja with Rauman Saaristokuljetus vessels M/V Apollonia and M/V Linnea. Home harbour: Marina Vista, Suvitie 14, 26100 Rauma. Remember to book your ferry separately from the cottage.

Hotel, restaurant, and beach café operate daily in peak summer (listed for 29.05–6.9, plus selected late-May and mid-September weekends). The island can also open for groups by arrangement in May and September.

  • Coordinates 61°8′40″N, 21°18′10″E
  • Setting Outermost Rauma island · Selkämeri National Park
  • Landmark Kylmäpihlaja lighthouse (36 m above sea level)
  • Ferry base Marina Vista, Suvitie 14, 26100 Rauma
