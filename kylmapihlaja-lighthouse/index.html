The island story

Where the light still watches the Gulf of Bothnia

Kylmäpihlaja is the outermost of Rauma’s large islands. Between 1952 and 1953 a combined lighthouse and pilot station rose here — twelve storeys, 104 steps, a square tower 36 m above the sea.

Today the lighthouse is in tourism use. Owned by the City of Rauma and operated by West Coast Seaservice, it offers rooms in the tower, a restaurant, café, saunas and a sheltered guest harbour — all within Selkämeri National Park.

Getting here

Rauma’s outermost island

Reachable by scheduled archipelago boats — remember to book ferry tickets as well as your room.

Archipelago setting

Kylmäpihlaja sits in Selkämeri National Park, the outermost of Rauma’s large islands — exposed to open sea, with a sheltered harbour basin (4 m depth) for guest boats.

Ferry access

Rauman Saaristokuljetus vessels M/V Apollonia and M/V Linnea run to the lighthouse. Home harbour: Marina Vista, Suvitie 14, 26100 Rauma.
