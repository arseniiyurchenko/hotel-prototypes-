### Location

Lingner Castle / Lingnerschloss, Bautzner Str. 132, 01099 Dresden, Germany. On the banks of the Elbe / Elbe slopes; views of Old Town & Elbe Valley; between Schloss Albrechtsberg and Schloss Eckberg (PDF); nearby Dresden Heide; tram/bus stop “Elbschlösser” (line 11 / EV11).

### Event / room spaces (labeled on site + PDF folder)

From website copy: historic setting; ballroom; Elbe terrace; champagne reception; colonnade; meadows; park grounds; civil weddings.

From **Tagungs- und Veranstaltungsmappe** PDF (linked from Meetings & Weddings pages):

1. **Festsaal** (EG) — 140 m² incl. winter garden + 26 m² side room; up to 150; €1,000 + extras  
2. **Lingnersalon** (EG) — 63 m²; up to 72; €350  
3. **Beletage / Sternensaal** (1. OG) — 88 m² + South balcony; lounges 45 m² each; up to 104 (+20 per lounge); Sternensaal €1,300; lounges €350 each  
4. **Kinosaal** (1. OG) — 63 m²; up to 72; €400 (civil ceremonies)  
5. **Dachplattform** — 230 m²; up to 100; €500 / with Turmstube Ost €700  
6. Outdoor / park events — summer open-air up to **400** persons (PDF)

### Amenities / features (verified)

Historic architecture & Kulturdenkmal; park / meadows / Elbe views; Dachplattform & Elbe terrace; catering partner Restaurant Lingnerterrassen; AV/conference equipment (projector, screen, mic, flipchart, concert grand, etc. per room); elevator & step-free access to 2nd floor; barrier-free toilets; three free parking spaces nearby; personal viewing & inquiry via STESAD rental; civil weddings with Dresden Registry Office; Friends Association cultural programme.

### Pricing / packages

Room **Nutzungsentgelt** listed in PDF (see above). Cleaning fee €120; heating surcharge €50/day in season; 19% VAT for companies. Sun–Thu negotiable. No packaged “wedding package” prices on the public pages beyond inquiry + PDF rentals.
