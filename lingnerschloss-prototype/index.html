Welcome to Lingner Castle Dresden

An architectural gem on the banks of the Elbe

The beautiful Lingner Castle is located directly on the banks of the Elbe River in Dresden — an architectural gem with a rich history.

Once the residence of visionary entrepreneur Karl August Lingner, who made Odol mouthwash famous worldwide, it now opens its doors for special events.

Whether it’s a wedding, family celebration, concert, conference or festive reception — experience special moments in a historic setting.

Lingner Castle · Bautzner Str. 132 · 01099 Dresden

Setting & services

What the castle offers

Features listed on the Lingnerschloss site and in the official event folder — nothing beyond what the venue publishes.

Historic architecture

19th-century Elbschloss and cultural monument — once home to Karl August Lingner, bequeathed to the city of Dresden.

Park, meadows & terrace

Wide park grounds on the Elbe slopes, meadows, colonnade, Elbe terrace and Dachplattform with views toward the Old Town and Saxon Switzerland.

Catering

In-house catering partner: Restaurant Lingnerterrassen. Gastronomy inquiries via the restaurant team (contacts published on the German site / PDF).

AV & conference equipment

Projectors, screens, microphones, flipcharts, concert grands, lecterns and room-specific sound — detailed per space in the event folder.

Access & parking

Step-free access by lift to the 2nd floor; barrier-free toilets. Three free parking spaces within walking distance; tram/bus “Elbschlösser” (line 11 / EV11).

Event coordination

Room rental via STESAD GmbH — personal advice, viewing appointments and individual offers on inquiry. Outdoor summer events up to 400 persons noted in the folder.

Weddings

Unforgettable moments in Lingner Castle

Nestled between meadows, the castle combines historic elegance with stunning views of the Old Town and the Elbe Valley — a truly magnificent setting.

Whether for a wedding, a champagne reception on the Elbe terrace, or a dinner in the ballroom, the Lingner Castle is the perfect venue.

Civil weddings — For civil ceremonies at Lingnerschloss, the Dresden Registry Office is the official partner. Date allocation is handled exclusively by the registry office.
