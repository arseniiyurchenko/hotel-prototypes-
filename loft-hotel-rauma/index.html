Location

Rauma by the sea

Finland’s third-oldest city — history and harbour life, with two World Heritage Sites and an easy reach by car, bus, or boat.

Old Rauma · UNESCO

The unique wooden Old Town World Heritage Site is only 2 km from the hotel.

Poroholma guest harbour

Arrive by boat — the marina is about 1 km away.

Active coastline

Nearby: gym, outdoor swimming pool, sandy beach, baseball and athletics fields. Ask reception about library, theatre, and events.

Connections

Convenient bus links on the Pori–Rauma and Turku–Rauma corridors; trains serve the larger cities.
