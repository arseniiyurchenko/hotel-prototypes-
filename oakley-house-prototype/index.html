Amenities

Everything you need on site

Features and services listed on the Oakley House website.

In-house catering

From tea and coffee to silver-service banqueting, with vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and other dietary options.

Fully stocked bar

Beer, wine, spirits, and soft drinks served by the team; corkage options available if you supply your own.

AV & wi-fi

Projectors, microphones, and fast wi-fi throughout the buildings for presentations and guest connectivity.

On-site parking

Car park on the grounds with space for 220 vehicles, accessed via the A21 (Bromley Common).

Pillar-free suite

Banqueting spaces free of pillars or obstructions, with air conditioning in the banqueting suite.

Event coordination

A front of house manager as your point of contact, plus an experienced team for food service and the bar.

Gardens & gazebo

Manicured lawns, landscaped gardens, and a licensed gazebo for ceremonies and photographs.

Live music & DJs

Recommendations and arrangements available; the venue is equipped with modern AV for performers.

Capacity

Up to 420 theatre-style for conferences; up to 320 seated for wedding receptions in the banqueting suite.

Note: Disabled / wheelchair access is not described on the source site — please enquire directly. A named dance floor is not listed as a separate amenity (the weddings page notes room to dance in the suite).

Location

How do I find Oakley House?

Conveniently positioned between London and Kent, just outside Bromley town centre on the A21 — rural feel, well connected.

Bromley BR2 8HA
