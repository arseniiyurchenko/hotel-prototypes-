Erith Town Centre

The perfect banqueting hall in London

The main hall of the P2 Events Centre Erith was designed in the 1930s and has a unique Art Deco charm. Situated in a prominent town-centre position at the junction of Pier Road and Queens Road (A206), opposite a large car park and close to Erith Riverside Shopping Centre.

Flexible space A mezzanine looks out over Erith Town Centre — adaptable for dinner, standing reception, wedding banquet, seminar, conference or exhibition.
Guest range Accommodating groups ranging from 80 to 600, with chefs offering menus to accompany the event.

Services & facilities

Everything covered, to your requirements

A dedicated events team delivers high-end, unique events — from hall hire in London to full event planning. Facilities and featured services below are listed on the live P2 Centre site.

Catering & bar

Chefs and menus for any cuisine and style, including African, Afro‑Caribbean, English and Asian foods, small chops and drinks — plus an on-site bar.

Car parking

Opposite the venue: a car park for 200–300 cars. Free for the first three hours, then 50p/hr. Free street parking evenings, weekends and Sundays.

Décor packages

Packages can include tablecloths, crockery and cutlery, table centre pieces, chair covers and sashes, and stage décor.

Event coordination

Full events planning with kitchen staff and security staff options, plus DJ/sound, photography, videography, cakes and more.

Comfort & connectivity

Internet access, Wi‑Fi and air conditioning across the venue.

Open daily

Weekdays, Saturday and Sunday — 9am to 12 midnight.

Packages: Full or part packages are available and tailor-made to each client. The live site does not publish fixed hall-hire prices — enquire for a quote. Dance floor is not mentioned on the source site, so it is not claimed here.
