Full-service accommodation under one roof

Do you need full-service accommodation in Rauma? Parimi Oy is a young local company offering tailored majoituspalvelu for companies and partners.

We own just over ten apartments, and through a partner network we can offer dozens of furnished homes of different sizes across Rauma city center — strongest selection in three- and four-room apartments, plus smaller units and detached houses.

Centralized availability & booking

One website for every apartment — live availability, guest details, and booking requests in one place instead of scattered messages.

  • Search across all locations from one booking form
  • Track occupancy for Basic, Premium & Gold packages
  • Manage guest stays and service schedules together
