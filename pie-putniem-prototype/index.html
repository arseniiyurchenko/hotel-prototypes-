Baložu namiņš

Named for the wood pigeons of Lapmežciems — and the Baloži family. Bedroom, kitchenette, terrace views into the trees.

From €100 / night · up to 4 guests

Lapmežciems

Between Kaņieris and the Gulf of Riga

Niedru iela 12, Lapmežciems, Tukuma novads — about 45 km from Riga, in a quiet forest fold between lake and sea.

  • Gulf of Riga beach — about an 8-minute walk
  • Kaņieris lake boat base, birdwatching tower & Riekstsala — about 8 minutes by car
  • Ķemeri National Park trails nearby (Zaļā kāpa, sulphur ponds, Great Ķemeri Bog boardwalk)
  • One of Latvia’s richest bird lakes — cabins named after local species
