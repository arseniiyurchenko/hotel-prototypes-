### Location

„Pītagi”, Košrags, Kolkas pagasts, Talsu novads, LV-3275. Within Slītere National Park, 800 m from the Baltic Sea. Liiv (Livonian) fishermen village on the Livonian Coast (Lībiešu krasts), Kurzeme / North Kurzeme. Nature trails, birds and wildlife.

### Room types (from /acommodations-lv)

1. **Kotedža (līdz 3 cilvēkiem)** — €70–80 / day (24 m²)
2. **Brīvdienu māja (1 guļamistaba, līdz 4)** — €90 / day (40 m²)
3. **Brīvdienu māja (2 guļamistabas, līdz 6)** — €140 / day (+ €10 per guest over 4)
4. **Brīvdienu māja (4 guļamistabas, līdz 8 + papildvietas)** — €220 / day (+ €10 extra bed; pirts €50 / 3 h)

Together: big holiday home + cottages + twin houses, up to ~50 people.

### Amenities (verified on site)

Kitchen / kitchenette, fireplace living room, sauna (pirts, paid extra on big house), covered terrace, private bathroom/shower, bedding & dishes included, Baltic Sea beach ~800 m, nature trails / guided hikes in Slītere & Livonian Coast, bird/wildlife watching.

**Not on site:** star rating, Wi‑Fi claim, parking policy, pet policy, grill, banquet-hall label — use neutral placeholders where needed; trust strip uses clearly marked placeholder testimonials.
