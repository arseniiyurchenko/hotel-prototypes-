Location

On the shore of Lake Talsi

Talsi — lake, hills & historic centre

Guest house Saule sits ashore Lake Talsi, close enough to town life while keeping a discreet, quiet atmosphere. From the terrace, guests look across the lake toward Talsi’s historic centre.

The house was built at the end of the 19th century at the foot of an ancient Couronian hillfort (pilskalns) on the lake shore — once part of Baron Fircks’ manor estate.
