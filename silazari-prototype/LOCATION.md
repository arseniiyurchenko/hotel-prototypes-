## Nearby landmarks (mentioned on live sources)

From **silazari.lv → Apkārtnē** and listing copy:

1. **Lāčupītes dendrārijs** (Lāčupīte Arboretum) — protected nature area between Apšuciems and Klapkalnciems; walking trail with local and exotic plants; path from the houses to the beach.
2. **Klapkalnciema pludmale** (Klapkalnciems beach) — Baltic Sea / Riga Bay shore; **~7 minutes’ walk** through the arboretum; summer beach bar in season.
3. **Klapkalnciema Zivis** — local fish market / café (fresh fish, popular soup).
4. **Ragaciems** — nearby dining: restaurants **“Ribas”** and **“Bermudas”**; Ragaciema tirdziņš (market).
5. **Apšuciems Beach** — ~2.9 km (listing distance).
