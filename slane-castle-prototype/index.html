Discover Slane Castle

Explore one of the most impressive historic buildings on the island of Ireland.

Home to the Conyngham family, exclusive weddings, famous annual concerts and Slane Irish Whiskey. A dream venue for family gatherings, weddings, gala dinners, conferences and corporate functions.

Location Slane, Co. Meath, Ireland — Boyne Valley, with the River Boyne and surrounding parkland as backdrop. Thirty-five minutes from Dublin Airport.

Venue spaces

Historic rooms, courtyard and estate

Spaces as labeled on the Slane Castle site — from the Neo-Gothic Ballroom to the Courtyard Marquee and full castle hire.

The Ballroom

Neo-Gothic Ballroom completed in 1821 for the visit of King George IV — marble statues and a stunning ceiling. Ideal for intimate banquets and events.

Up to 106 guests

The Great Hall

Spacious hall developed in the 18th century, designed by James Wyatt and Francis Johnston — suited to a champagne welcome reception.

120 standing · 80 theatre

Adjacent ground floor rooms

Morning Room, Drawing Room and Dining Room — adjacent to the Great Hall and Ballroom, with easy one-way flow.

50 standing · 25 seated

The Dining Room

Hosts a magnificent 15ft mahogany table — suited to boardroom-style meetings, working lunches or breakout sessions.

Weddings at the estate

Exclusive use for your wedding day

Together we will create the wedding of your dreams. Slane Castle offers each couple exclusive use of this beautiful venue, with flexible packages for 15 up to 250 guests.

  • Castle Ballroom weddings — up to 106 guests
  • Courtyard Marquee weddings — 107–250 guests, with full use of the Main Floor for arrival and evening entertainment
  • Intimate weddings from 15 guests, with bespoke packages
  • Optional exclusive weekend hire: Arrival Day, Main Event, and a Perfect Ending gathering on the estate
  • The Castle Collection — seven spacious bedrooms accommodating up to 17 guests; additional stays on the Estate and nearby in Slane village
