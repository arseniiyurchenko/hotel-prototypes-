Location

Ragaciems, between sea and lake

On the Gulf of Riga coast — the narrow strip between the beach and Lake Kaņieris, in Lapmežciems parish.

Viesu nams “Smaidas” sits on Ikvildu gatve in Ragaciems. The site places the house 350 m from the sea and 250 m from Lake Kaņieris, about 10 km from Kauguri.

  • Famous Ragaciema zivju tirdziņš (fish market) — a short walk
  • Fish restaurant “Bermudas” and café “Lindaga” nearby
  • Lapmežciema muzejs ~3 km; lighthouse ~2 km
  • White sandy beach; quieter stretch toward Kolka
  • Bus stops toward Riga and Kolka close by
