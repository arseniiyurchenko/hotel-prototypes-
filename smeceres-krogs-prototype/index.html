Vidzeme

Location

Near Madona, in Smeceres sils valley

Near Madona, in the Smeceres sils valley (Smeceres sila ielejā), by the roadside — always welcoming at Smeceres Krogs. About 147 km from Rīga; Madona is roughly 5 km away according to guest reviews on the site.

A natural base for forest walks, sports at Smeceres sils, and countryside rest in Mārciena parish.
