Welcome

A premier luxury events venue in Bletchley, Milton Keynes

The Palatial Hall is a premier luxury events venue based in Bletchley, Milton Keynes, designed to host unforgettable weddings, celebrations, corporate events, conferences, large gatherings, and special occasions.

Combining elegance, versatility, and exceptional service, the venue offers a stunning contemporary setting with spacious event halls, stylish interiors, professional lighting, stage facilities, and flexible event layouts.

The Layout

The Crystal Ballroom

Our breath-taking hall, The Crystal Ballroom, makes for a stunning grand affair. Boasting a capacity from 50–500 people seated, the banquet hall is perfect for your Wedding Breakfast — and for dancing the night away under exquisite chandeliers, surrounded by the lighting system.

  • 100–400 Seated lunches and dinners with dance floor
  • 100–500 Seated lunches and dinners without dance floor
  • 100–500 Parties and receptions
  • 100–600 Meetings, conferences, standing barbecues & buffets

Site note: additional named banqueting suites beyond The Crystal Ballroom were not labelled on the official pages reviewed. Bridal suites and dressing rooms are listed among venue services.

How to find us

Bletchley, Milton Keynes
