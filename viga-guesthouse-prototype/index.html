Pāvilosta

On Latvia’s west coast

Harbor town, grey dunes, and the Baltic Sea — where the river Saka meets the sea.

Pelēko kāpu pilsēta — Pāvilosta

Guest house VIGA sits in a small harbor town on Latvia’s west coast, at the mouth of the river Saka into the Baltic Sea — the grey-dune town of Pāvilosta. The white sandy beach is only 300 m away.

Owners Inta and Varis Vīganti welcome guests year-round to a warm, home-like stay by the sea.

  • Address — Viļņu iela 3, Pāvilosta, LV-3466, Latvia
  • Landmarks — Baltic Sea beach, harbor town, river Saka estuary, grey dunes
  • Coordinates — 56.88914, 21.177017
