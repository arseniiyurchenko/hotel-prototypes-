Where we are

Villa Anna is a small seaside hotel right by the water in Apšuciems — ideal for quiet stays, beach walks, and sunrise breakfasts on the terrace.

  • Apšuciems, Tukuma novads, Engures pagasts
  • Located right by the sea, with views of the waves and beach
  • Evening walks along the coast
