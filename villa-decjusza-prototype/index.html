Find us

Location

West of Kraków’s historic centre, in the green district of Wola Justowska.

Wola Justowska, Kraków

Villa Decius stands in a palace-and-park complex named after Justus Decius. The grounds include Park Decjusza — one of the oldest parks in Kraków — with gardens once arranged in Gothic, Romantic and English styles.

A quiet setting with room to host conferences and banquets away from the city bustle, while remaining in Kraków.
