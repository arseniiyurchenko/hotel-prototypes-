Event spaces

Ceremony, dining, dancing, and grounds

Spaces as labeled on the Wool Tower site — from the vaulted ceremony room to Wool Hall and the riverside grounds.

Your ceremony

Vaulted ceremony space

South-facing sunlight through Georgian windows, vaulted ceilings, exposed brick and stone. Fully licensed for religious and civil ceremonies and partnerships of up to 200 guests.

Dining

Wool Hall

An open-plan room bathed in natural light, with pitch pine beams, exposed brick walls, quarry stone floors, Georgian windows, and a Juliet balcony overlooking the courtyard. Banquet or circular tables for 25 to 200 guests.

Its party time

Dancefloor

Cast iron columns, vaulted brick ceilings, exposed stone walls and polished floors. Space for your DJ or band against a backlit stone wall, with evening food and drinks — celebrating your day your way.

Roam freely

Riverside grounds & courtyard

With no public or membership access, the river and ground are completely yours. Acres of private natural countryside, a sheltered courtyard for evening sun, plus firepits and blanket boxes for a cosy end to the day.

Amenities & features

Exclusive use, curated service

Drawn from the Wool Tower website FAQs, homepage, and conference brochure hosted on wooltower.com.

  • Exclusive use One wedding per day — river and grounds reserved for your party.
  • Capacity Weddings from 25 to 200 guests; no minimum numbers. Conferencing up to 200 delegates.
  • Licensed ceremonies Religious and civil ceremonies and partnerships; ceremony included in venue hire.
  • Catering Hand-chosen meals; in-house caterer Caulfield & Dorrity. Own caterers permitted with certification.
  • Coordination Wedding coordinators, styling evenings, and a dedicated Conferencing & Events Manager.
  • Parking Two large car parks outside the venue, suitable for coach drop-offs. Complimentary on-site parking for conferences.
  • Bar & music Venue hire typically 10am–1.30am; bar & music stop at 1am (midnight Sundays). House wines available; own alcohol not permitted.
  • Conference kit Unlimited Wi-Fi, flip chart & markers, pads & pens. Audio visual equipment available at additional cost.
  • Stay nearby Accommodation via sister company Raceview Mill — Riverside Wedding Suite, riverside suites, Black Sheep Cottage & The Hen House (up to 30 adults).

Our story

Family and love at the heart of the mill

Raceview Mill dates back to 1806. After closure and a near-demolition, a local family restored The Wool Tower — a father and daughter team bringing the building back to life. Family and love sit at the heart of the venue.
